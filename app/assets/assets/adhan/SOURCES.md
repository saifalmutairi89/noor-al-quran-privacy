# Adhan sounds

The prayer-notification sounds, cut from freely licensed recordings on
Wikimedia Commons. Changes: a single excerpt of each (nothing spliced),
loudness evened out, a short fade at the ends, and conversion to the
formats each phone needs (CAF for iOS, Ogg Vorbis for Android, AAC for
listening in the app).

- `adhan.*` — the first 29.5 seconds of "Beautiful adhan" by Adam-synagda,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beautiful_adhan.ogg, CC0 1.0
  (public domain dedication).
- `adhan_fajr.*` — "as-salatu khayrun min an-nawm" (said twice), 3:08–3:27
  of "Eid al-Fitr Fajr azan at Malmö Mosque - 19 August 2012" by Islamic
  Center Malmö,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eid_al-Fitr_Fajr_azan_at_Malm%C3%B6_Mosque_-_19_August_2012.webm,
  licensed CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

The Android copies are in android/app/src/main/res/raw/.
